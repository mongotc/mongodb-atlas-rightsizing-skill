## Why this matters

Tier availability varies by region. A tier appearing in `GCPHardwareSpec20240805.instanceSize` only means Atlas knows the name — this endpoint says *where* you can actually provision it today. `src/constants/{aws,gcp,azure}_tiers.json` uses this payload to populate the `regions[]` field per row.
